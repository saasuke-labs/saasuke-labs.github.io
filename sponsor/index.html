Sponsor

Keep the tools you rely on maintained.

Gengo, Nagare and Kotomi are built and maintained independently, in the open. If one of them saves your team time, sponsoring is the most direct way to make sure it keeps getting releases, fixes and documentation.

What sponsorship pays for

Maintenance

Bug fixes, dependency updates and signed, checksummed releases for every platform, on a steady cadence. See the release notes for the track record.

Documentation

Guides, references and worked examples for each project, so new users get to a working setup without reading the source.

Infrastructure

Hosting for the public demos and playgrounds, CI minutes and the release pipeline behind every binary and container image.

What sponsors get

Sponsorship is support for the work, not a purchase. Still, companies that fund the projects should get something useful back.

Visibility

Your logo and a link on this site and in the README of the projects you back, in front of the developers who use them.

A direct line

Your issues and questions get looked at first, and you can tell us which features would matter most to your team.

Commercial licensing

Gengo is free for non-commercial use. A commercial licence lets you use it inside a product or service. Licence details.
